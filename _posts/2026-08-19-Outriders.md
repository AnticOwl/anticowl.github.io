## How To
### Use The Cheat Table
Once you have installed Cheat Engine 7.6 or higher (if not already done), double-click the cheat table file you downloaded. Check the `Auto Attach to Game` box. Cheat Engine will automatically connect to the game when detected.

Press `Page Down` to enable the camera structure and leave it enabled.

### Take A Screenshot
* Press `Numpad 0` to pause the game.
* Press `Delete` to disable the pause menu.
* Press `Insert` to detach the camera.
* Frame and take your shot.
* Disable the options in reverse order to return to the game.

### Use IGCS Relay
The table includes native **IGCS Relay** support for camera-based DoF rendering with compatible backends such as **IGCSDOF** and **MARTY'S MODS Parallax DOF**.

* Install ReShade with add-on support and [IGCS Relay](https://anticowl.github.io/2026/08/12/IGCSRelay/).
* Press `Page Down` to enable the camera structure and leave it enabled.
* Press `Insert` to detach the camera.
* Press `End` to enable **IGCS Relay support** in the table.
* Open the ReShade overlay and check that the IGCS Relay status is **OK**.
* Choose your DOF backend, frame your shot and start the render.

During an IGCS Relay render session, the Cheat Engine freecam temporarily ignores its own keyboard and controller input. This prevents accidental camera movement while still leaving the ReShade controls available.

When the render is finished, the camera is restored to its original position.
